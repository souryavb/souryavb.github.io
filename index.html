About me

I'm a Data Science and Business Administration student at Northeastern's Khoury College, graduating in May 2028. The two halves of that degree pull in useful directions: the data science side teaches me to check whether something is true, and the business side keeps me asking whether it matters to anyone.

The work I've been proudest of so far has been unglamorous. At the Connecticut Judicial Branch I spent a summer reading help desk email, and the pattern that stood out wasn't a clever security problem — it was that urgent requests sat in the same queue as routine ones until somebody got to them. At Bain Capital it was a morning meeting that every employee assembled by hand. Neither is a hard technical problem. Both were worth fixing, and fixing them meant understanding a workflow well enough that the people living in it trusted the replacement.

Outside of coursework I play club rugby, and I'm a member of the Finance and Investment Club and ASME.
