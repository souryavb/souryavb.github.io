About me

I'm a Data Science and Business student at Northeastern University, concentrating in Finance and graduating in 2028. I care about the part of finance where a claim can be checked: if a strategy is supposed to work, the data should be able to say so.

That's what pulled me into backtesting. It's easy to describe a trading idea that sounds reasonable and much harder to show it survives transaction costs, drawdowns, and the stretches where it simply doesn't work. Building my own engine meant I couldn't hide behind a tidy summary statistic — I had to look at every trade it took and explain the bad ones.
